## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Gujrat, building at the intersection of mobile engineering and applied AI. My work spans **Flutter**, the **full-stack web** (React/Node), and **machine learning**, with a focus on shipping products that are polished enough to hand to a real user — not just tutorials.

I'm currently:

- 🎓 Completing my **BS in Computer Science**
- 📱 Building scalable, production-quality apps as a **Flutter Developer**
- 🌐 Working across the stack as a **Full Stack Developer**
- 🤖 Deepening my skills as an **AI & Machine Learning Enthusiast**
- 🧠 Exploring how **AI can solve real, everyday problems** — not just power demos
- 💼 Preparing my portfolio for **Software Engineering & AI internships**

I care about clean architecture, honest documentation, and code that still makes sense to me six months later.
